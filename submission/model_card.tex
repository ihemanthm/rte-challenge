{\Large\bfseries Model card: distilled, pruned DeBERTa-v3-xsmall for RTE}\\[2pt]
{\small [Student name] $\cdot$ [Roll number] $\cdot$ CS6315E RTE Challenge \\ code: submitted alongside (see the README)}

\textbf{Architecture.} \texttt{microsoft/deberta-v3-xsmall} encoder (12 layers, hidden 384, 6 heads) + pooler + linear 2-way head. Full fine-tuning with knowledge distillation from a DeBERTa-v3-base teacher (masked KD, below); \textbf{no} LoRA/adapters. After training, the input word-embedding is \emph{vocabulary-pruned}: only the rows for the 62,245 kept tokens (special tokens $\cup$ every training-set token $\cup$ the 60,000 most frequent tokens of the tokenizer's unigram model) are kept; any other token id maps to \texttt{[UNK]} through a fixed lookup table.

\textbf{Training data.} RTE train (2,490 pairs; identical to GLUE RTE train), minus a 10\% validation split grouped by premise. Teacher soft labels are out-of-fold logits of five DeBERTa-v3-base models trained on the same train set. No external NLI/RTE data, no pre-fine-tuned RTE checkpoints. Dev (GLUE RTE validation, 277) is used only for reporting.

\textbf{Training hyperparameters.}\\[2pt]
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Loss & $0.5\,\mathrm{CE} + 0.5\,T^2\,\mathrm{KL}$, $T{=}2$; pure CE where the teacher is wrong \\
Optimiser & AdamW, weight decay 0.01 (none on bias/LayerNorm), grad-clip 1.0 \\
Learning rate & $5\times10^{-5}$ top layer, layer-wise decay 0.9 per layer down; head $1\times10^{-4}$ \\
Schedule & linear warm-up 10\%, then linear decay \\
Batch size / max length & 16 / 256 tokens (premise truncated first) \\
Epochs & up to 15, early stopping on validation accuracy, patience 4 \\
Seeds & 13, 21, 42, 87, 100; submitted = best val.\ of 20 runs (seed 21, epoch 7) \\
Precision & fp32 weights; bf16 autocast on CUDA, fp32 elsewhere \\
Pruning & post-hoc; top-$N$ chosen on train only (98\% coverage) \\
Hardware / software & Colab T4 GPU (reruns bit-identical); PyTorch 2.14, Transformers 5.22.2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Intended use and limits.} Binary entailment on short English news-style pairs. Text with tokens outside the kept vocabulary loses those tokens to \texttt{[UNK]} (98.5\% of dev tokens are kept; on the 60\% of dev pairs that are fully covered, outputs equal the unpruned model exactly). Weak on high-overlap non-entailments (36.8\% error), numbers (31.2\%), modality.
