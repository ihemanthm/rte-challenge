{\Large\bfseries Model card: three DeBERTa-v3-xsmall models for RTE}\\[2pt]
{\small [Student name] $\cdot$ [Roll number] $\cdot$ CS6315E RTE Challenge \\ code: submitted alongside (see the README)}

\textbf{Architecture (shared by all three).} \texttt{microsoft/deberta-v3-xsmall} encoder (12 layers, hidden 384, 6 heads) + pooler + linear 2-way head; full fine-tuning, \textbf{no} LoRA/adapters. Model B additionally has a \emph{vocabulary-pruned} input embedding: only the rows of 62,245 tokens are kept (special tokens $\cup$ every training-set token $\cup$ the 60,000 most frequent tokens of the tokenizer's unigram model); other token ids map to \texttt{[UNK]} through a fixed lookup table (int32, 0.5 MB, not a parameter).

{\small All three: 21,640,322 non-embedding parameters; word embedding $128{,}100\times384 = 49{,}190{,}400$ (A, C) or $62{,}245\times384 = 23{,}902{,}080$ (B). All parameters trainable. Val: premise-grouped 10\% split of train (B: with only the fit split's tokens, so the text is unseen).}

\textbf{Why these three.} A has the highest validation accuracy of all 20 xsmall checkpoints (4 methods $\times$ 5 seeds). B removes the 36\% of parameters that sit in embedding rows RTE rarely uses, at an estimated 0--3 points. C is the best checkpoint trained without the teacher, so its errors differ from A's. Accuracy first: A; accuracy per parameter: B.

\textbf{Training data.} RTE train (2,490 pairs; identical to GLUE RTE train), minus the validation split. Teacher soft labels (A, B) are out-of-fold logits of five DeBERTa-v3-base models trained on the same data. No external NLI/RTE data, no LLM-generated data, no pre-fine-tuned RTE checkpoints. Dev (GLUE RTE validation, 277) is used only for reporting.

\textbf{Training hyperparameters.}\\[2pt]
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Loss & A, B: $0.5\,\mathrm{CE} + 0.5\,T^2\,\mathrm{KL}$, $T{=}2$, pure CE where the teacher is wrong; C: CE \\
Optimiser & AdamW, weight decay 0.01, grad-clip 1.0 \\
Learning rate & $5\times10^{-5}$ top layer, layer-wise decay 0.9; head $1\times10^{-4}$ \\
Schedule / batch / length & 10\% linear warm-up then decay / 16 / 256 tokens \\
Epochs & up to 15, early stopping on validation accuracy, patience 4 \\
Precision / hardware & bf16 autocast on Colab T4 (reruns bit-identical); PyTorch 2.14, Transformers 5.22.2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Reference.} DeBERTa-v3-base (teacher), 184.4M parameters: dev $84.8 \pm 1.1$ over 5 seeds.

\textbf{Limits.} Short English news-style pairs. Weak on high-overlap non-entailments, numbers and modality. B loses tokens outside its vocabulary to \texttt{[UNK]} (98.5\% of dev tokens kept).

\textbf{Run.} \texttt{python scripts/predict\_test.py --input <test file>} runs all three (README).
